Projection mechanism references (code only; no upstream artwork reused):

Compatibility-Friendly Texture Projection, BluntBSE, published May 22, 2026.
https://godotshaders.com/shader/compatibility-friendly-texture-projection/
The publisher marks its shader code CC0. Adapted depth reconstruction, bounded
volume masking, additive texture/alpha and soft falloff mechanisms.

Fake light projection, Rytelier, published May 11, 2026.
https://godotshaders.com/shader/fake-light-projection/
The publisher marks its shader code MIT. Adapted normal-buffer sampling and
incidence mechanism; perspective, spaces, transforms and bounds are Peraviz's.
